title: What Jay Gatsby Had For Breakfast
year: 2026
description: A 'poem a clef' inspired by The Great Gatsby
---

    Two eggs, over hard (like my life, he thinks)
    Not green (that’d be too obvious)
    He considers adding hot sauce on top

    A side of bacon (not ham, but from Chicago)
    He always starts with that
    Two waffles (he doesn’t touch them)

    Black coffee (it’s not coffee if you add anything)
    The smell helps him to wake up
    He hates the taste (prefers to drink tea)

    Doesn’t even glance at the plate of pancakes
    Two of them arranged side-by-side (not in a stack)
    The maple syrup also forgotten

    He reaches for a glass of juice
    Knocks it over instead (distracted by the coffee)
    He isn’t usually so careless

    It spills all over the eggs
    He was just about to take another bite
    Suddenly he isn’t hungry anymore
